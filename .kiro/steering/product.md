# Product context

Stivium is a BNB Chain AI agent marketplace. Its user promise is: compare risk and track-record signals, activate an agent with a spend cap and scoped permissions, and revoke it without handing over a blank check.

Keep the four catalog categories at equal depth: rebalancing, grid trading, yield optimisation, and health-factor monitoring. Preserve a deterministic seeded catalog so a judge can complete the main flow even when an external data source is unavailable.

When describing integrations, distinguish local policy activation, BNB testnet Altana session keys, wallet and swap connectivity, and the mocked x402 payment path. Never imply that a mocked or optional path is live.
